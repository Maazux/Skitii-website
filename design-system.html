Typography

Manrope, one family at weights 400 to 800. Inter is the fallback. Swap in the official brand font if supplied. Sizes use fluid clamp values, so one scale serves desktop and mobile. Body line length stays under 70 characters.

Max content width 1160px. Side gutters 40px on desktop, 32px on mobile. Breakpoints: 960px (tablet, two columns become one) and 720px (mobile, menu button, sticky call-to-action).

Elevation

Shadows only on the hero visual card and the enquiry form. Everything else uses a 1px border.

Visuals and motion

Imagery

Use approved product photography and realistic oncology settings. No distressing patient images, no invented hardware, no generic wellness stock. Where a photo is missing, use a dashed placeholder labelled with its subject.

Icons and data graphics

Rounded line icons, 2px stroke, 24px grid. Data graphics are restrained: one teal line on navy, labelled "Illustrative" unless it is real, approved data.

Motion

One orchestrated moment per page: the heartbeat trace draws in over about 4 seconds. Hover and focus changes take 200ms. Everything respects reduced-motion settings and nothing autoplays with sound.

Accessibility floor

Visible keyboard focus on every control, skip link, 48px tap targets, semantic headings, alt text or aria-labels on every graphic, colour never the only signal, tested at 200% zoom.

Language and claims

Clinical and evidence-conscious, calm, never sensational. HRV is a physiological signal used for adaptation, not a diagnosis of emotion.

Use

  • “Investigational HRV-adaptive music therapy device”
  • “Designed to support anxiety management in oncology care”
  • “CDSCO Class B licensing in progress”
  • “ISO 13485:2016 certified” (quality management certification)
  • “Illustrative graphic. Not patient data.”

Avoid unless an approved Skitii source supports it

  • FDA approved, CE certified, or any approval not yet granted
  • “Reduces anxiety”, “proven”, “guaranteed”, “zero side effects”
  • “Detects emotion” or “diagnoses stress”
  • Cure, treat or prevent claims
  • Statistics with no source link
